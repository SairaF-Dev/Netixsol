# Teacher guide

The teacher area shows how the class is doing and who needs help. It also collects everything
that needs a human decision. **The platform suggests; you decide.** No mark is lowered, no
question is retired and no student is penalised without a teacher's decision.

Log in with the teacher account. The menu has six pages.

## Class overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Top row** | students in class, active this week, at risk of dropping out (high and medium), average concepts mastered, items waiting in the review queue |
| **Weakest concepts** | the concepts the class knows least, among those at least 20% of the class has practised. These are good topics for the next live session |
| **Mastery by section and concept** | a heatmap; each cell is the share of a section that has mastered a concept, from "Few know it" to "Most know it". Look for a whole column that is pale (a hard concept for everyone), or a pale row (a section falling behind) |

## At risk

These are the students the dropout warning flags this week.

| level | what to do | how often these students drop out |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | call within 24 hours | about 39% |
| **Medium** | a personal message in the app or on WhatsApp | about 21% |

**Each row shows:**

- the **reason in words**, for example "struggling: several wrong answers in a row";
- the **Suggested action** for that reason;
- the last answer, and the student's section and mastery.

Filter by level or reason. Press **Mark contacted** when you have reached a student. It is saved
in this browser only.

The warning is as good as the visible activity allows: the high and medium groups together
hold about 47% of the students who will leave. A student who is not flagged can still
leave. Use it to plan outreach, never to label a student.

## Review queue

Everything that needs your judgement is here, and every decision is saved with your name and an
optional note.

| item | why it is here | your options |
|---|---|---|
| **Possible copying: needs teacher review** | two submissions to the same exercise are unusually similar. You see both programs side by side, with matching lines marked | Not copying · Talk to both students · Escalate |
| **Low mark to check** | a mark under 4. Tests can miss a good idea, such as an off-by-one loop or printing instead of returning | Keep the mark · Raise the mark · Talk to the student |
| **Output imitated a test result** | the program printed something that looks like the grader's output (often harmless debugging, sometimes an attempt to fake a pass; the mark is never affected by it) | Harmless output · Talk to the student · Escalate |

**On copying:**

- **Similar is not proof.** Short exercises have few ways to be written. Compare the structure
  and the unusual details, not just the overlap.
- **The queue shows the top pairs of each exercise**, not every pair over a line. Your work stays
  the same whatever the class size, and you see the most similar pairs first.

## Question bank

| | |
|---|---|
| **Questions to retire** | questions where strong and weak students do about the same, or strong students do worse: usually a wrong key or a confusing wording. The evidence is shown with each |
| **Difficulty to relabel** | the author's level is 2 or more away from the level students' answers show. Choose the new level |
| **Generated questions waiting for review** | AI-drafted questions that passed the automatic checks; they are used only after review |

Your choice for each question: **Retire**, **Relabel** or **Keep**.

## Tutor safety

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evaluation runs** | the tutor's test results: answers given away, jailbreaks resisted, correctness, teaching and tone |
| **In the app** | live counts: questions asked, hints given by rung, leaks caught before sending, the share in UrduLish, how many students used it |

You cannot read students' conversations: they are not stored (see
`docs/tutor_safety_policy.md`). If a student reports a bad reply, ask for a screenshot and follow
the incident steps in that policy.

## Impact

What the platform changes, and how sure we are. Every section names the report its numbers come
from.

| section | what it shows |
|---|---|
| **Adaptive path vs one fixed path** | simulated students: dropout lower, but fewer concepts known at the end |
| **Teacher time saved by auto-grading** | a calculator. Change the minutes you spend marking one submission and the share you still review, to fit your school |
| **Dropout early warning, Code grading, Safety** | how well each part works, with its limits |
| **Real-student A/B test** | the plan for testing the adaptive path on real students, and its rehearsal. Not run yet |

## Good habits

- **Check the review queue after each deadline.** Low marks are where a good idea most often went
  unrewarded.
- **Use the heatmap to pick the next live session's topic.**
- **Contact high-risk students the same day.** Use the suggested action as a starting point,
  not a script.
