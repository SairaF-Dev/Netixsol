## 2. Doctor Consultation Fee Structure
Consultation fees depend on the doctor's seniority and specialty:
* **General Medicine**: PKR 2,000 per consultation.
* **Paediatrics**: PKR 2,500 per consultation.
* **Dermatology**: PKR 2,500 per consultation.
* **ENT**: PKR 2,500 per consultation.
* **Gynaecology**: PKR 3,000 per consultation.
* **Cardiology**: PKR 3,500 - 4,000 per consultation.

## 3. Appointment & Rescheduling Policy
* Patients can book, reschedule, or cancel appointments up to 2 hours before the scheduled time.
* Same-day walk-in appointments are available for urgent non-emergency cases, but booking in advance is recommended to avoid wait times.
* Rescheduling is free of charge if done at least 2 hours in advance.

## 4. Emergency & Triage Policy
* City Care Clinics is an outpatient facility. We do NOT handle severe life-threatening trauma or critical emergency surgeries on-site.
* Any patient presenting with red-flag symptoms (severe chest pain, stroke signs, difficulty breathing, heavy uncontrolled bleeding, loss of consciousness) is immediately referred to the nearest tertiary hospital ER or emergency services (1122).
