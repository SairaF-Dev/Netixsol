# Symptom to Specialty Routing Rules

This table establishes the routing criteria for matching patient symptoms to medical specialties at City Care Clinics.

| Specialty | Primary Symptoms & Referral Reasons | Emergency Red Flags (Escalate to ER) |
|-----------|-------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Cardiology** | Mild chest tightness, palpitations, high blood pressure check, shortness of breath on exertion, swollen ankles | Severe crushing chest pain radiating to jaw/left arm, acute breathlessness, fainting/syncope |
| **Paediatrics** | Child (<12 yrs) fever, cough, vomiting, diarrhea, skin rash in infant, poor feeding, growth checkup | Infant (<3 months) fever > 100.4°F, lethargy, seizures, severe breathing distress in child |
| **Gynaecology** | Irregular menstrual cycles, pregnancy routine checkup, pelvic pain, morning sickness, menopause symptoms | Severe sudden abdominal/pelvic pain in pregnant woman, heavy vaginal bleeding, pregnancy eclampsia |
| **Dermatology** | Skin rash, acne, eczema, fungal infection, hair loss, allergic skin reaction, itching, mole check | Sudden severe facial swelling / hives with throat tightness (Anaphylaxis -> ER) |
| **ENT** | Earache, ear discharge, sore throat, sinus pressure, nasal congestion, tonsillitis, hearing loss, dizziness | Foreign object trapped in throat/airway, severe neck stiffness with high fever |
| **General Medicine** | Routine fever, mild cough, body aches, fatigue, gastric acidity, constipation, general checkup, blood sugar review | Unconsciousness, stroke symptoms (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty) |
