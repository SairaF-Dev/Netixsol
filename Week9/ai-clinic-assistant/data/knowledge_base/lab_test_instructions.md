# City Care Clinics - Lab Test Preparation Instructions

## 1. Fasting Blood Glucose (FBG) & Lipid Profile
* **Fasting Requirement**: Requires strict overnight fasting of 8 to 12 hours before sample collection.
* **Allowed**: Plain water is allowed.
* **Not Allowed**: Tea, coffee, milk, juices, chewing gum, smoking, or any food during the fasting period.
* **Medication**: Take morning blood pressure/routine medicines with water unless advised otherwise by your physician. Diabetes medicine should be taken AFTER blood sample collection.

## 2. Complete Blood Count (CBC)
* **Fasting Requirement**: No fasting required. Sample can be collected at any time of day.
* **Instructions**: Stay hydrated by drinking normal amounts of water.

## 3. HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin)
* **Fasting Requirement**: No fasting required.
* **Instructions**: Can be performed at any time regardless of meals. Measures average blood sugar control over the past 3 months.

## 4. Liver Function Tests (LFT) & Serum Creatinine (Renal Profile)
* **Fasting Requirement**: 4 to 6 hours of mild fasting recommended for optimal accuracy.
* **Instructions**: Avoid heavy fatty meals or alcohol 24 hours prior to the test.

## 5. Abdominal & Pelvic Ultrasound
* **Fasting Requirement**: 6 to 8 hours fasting required for upper abdominal ultrasound (to visualize gallbladder clearly).
* **Urinary Bladder Requirement**: For pelvic ultrasound, full bladder is mandatory. Drink 3-4 glasses of water 1 hour before test and do not urinate until exam is complete.
