# Zarya Digital Bank — Schedule of Charges (SOC)
Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Document Ref: SOC-2026-Q4

## 1. Inter-Bank Funds Transfer (IBFT) & Raast
- **Raast P2P (Person to Person) Transfers**: Free of charge (Rs. 0) for all amounts up to daily transaction limit.
- **IBFT via 1LINK / Mobile App (Up to Rs. 25,000 per month)**: Free of charge (Rs. 0).
- **IBFT via 1LINK / Mobile App (Exceeding Rs. 25,000 per month)**: Flat fee of **Rs. 50 per transaction** plus applicable FED / provincial sales tax. (Note: Previously Rs. 25, updated as per Circular SBP-PSD-04/2026).
- **Intra-bank Transfers (Zarya to Zarya)**: Completely free (Rs. 0) unlimited.

## 2. Debit Card Issuance & Annual Maintenance Fees
- **PayPak Standard Chip Card**: Rs. 1,800 per annum.
- **PayPak EMV Contactless Card**: Rs. 2,200 per annum.
- **Visa Classic Debit Card**: Rs. 2,800 per annum.
- **Virtual Debit Card (In-App)**: Rs. 0 (Free generation for first card).
- **Card Replacement (Lost, Stolen or Damaged)**: Rs. 1,200 (PayPak), Rs. 1,800 (Visa).

## 3. ATM Cash Withdrawal Charges
- **Zarya Digital Bank ATMs**: Free of charge (Rs. 0).
- **1LINK / MNET Member Bank ATMs (Off-Us)**: Rs. 23.44 per cash withdrawal transaction.
- **ATM Balance Inquiry (Off-Us 1LINK ATMs)**: Rs. 3.13 per inquiry.

## 4. SMS Alerts & Digital Services
- **Mandatory Digital Transaction Alerts (Email & Push Notification)**: Free of charge (Rs. 0).
- **SMS Alert Subscription Fee**: Rs. 150 + tax per month.
- **Duplicate Account Statement (Physical Paper Print)**: Rs. 50 per statement.
- **E-Statement via Registered Email**: Free of charge (Rs. 0).
