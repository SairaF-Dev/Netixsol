# Zarya Digital Bank — Complaint Resolution & Turnaround Times (TAT)
Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Document Ref: TAT-SBP-BCPG-2026

Compliant with State Bank of Pakistan Banking Conduct & Consumer Protection Guidelines.

## 1. Disputed ATM Cash Deductions
- **Description**: Account debited at ATM, but cash was partially or fully not dispensed.
- **TAT (Turnaround Time)**: **2 to 3 business days** (48 to 72 working hours) following 1LINK electronic switch reconciliation.
- **Immediate Action**: Temporary provisional credit posted if reconciliation exceeds 5 business days.

## 2. Inter-Bank Funds Transfer (IBFT) / Raast Failures
- **Description**: Funds deducted from sender's Zarya account but not credited to recipient beneficiary bank.
- **TAT (Turnaround Time)**: **24 hours** for standard 1LINK IBFT; **4 hours** for Raast instant clearing.

## 3. Disputed Point of Sale (POS) / E-Commerce Transactions
- **Description**: Merchant charged twice or merchandise not delivered.
- **TAT (Turnaround Time)**: **7 to 14 business days** for domestic PayPak transactions; up to **30 calendar days** for international Visa chargeback disputes.

## 4. Fraudulent / Unauthorized Account Activity
- **Description**: Unauthorized card debit, SIM swap fraud, phishing transfer.
- **TAT (Turnaround Time)**: Immediate account / card freeze within **5 minutes** of helpline notification. Formal forensic investigation report completed within **14 calendar days**.
- **Customer Escalation**: Automatically escalated to Zarya Bank Fraud Management Unit (FMU) and SBP Banking Mohtasib liaison.
