# Zarya Digital Bank — Transaction & Channel Limits Policy
Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Document Ref: LIM-2026-V3

## 1. Zarya Asaan Digital Account (Level 1)
- **Daily Cash Withdrawal (ATM)**: Up to Rs. 50,000 per day.
- **Daily Funds Transfer (IBFT & Raast combined)**: Up to Rs. 100,000 per day.
- **Daily POS / Merchant Purchase**: Up to Rs. 100,000 per day.
- **Monthly Total Credit / Debit Turnover Limit**: Rs. 500,000 per calendar month.
- **International E-Commerce**: Not permitted on Level 1 as per State Bank of Pakistan regulations. Upgrade to Full KYC required.

## 2. Zarya Digital Freelancer & Full KYC Account (Level 2)
- **Daily Cash Withdrawal (ATM)**: Up to Rs. 100,000 per day (maximum Rs. 25,000 per single ATM transaction).
- **Daily Funds Transfer (IBFT & Raast combined)**: Up to Rs. 500,000 per day (can be enhanced to Rs. 1,000,000 upon 2FA biometric confirmation).
- **Daily POS Purchase (In-Store)**: Up to Rs. 250,000 per day.
- **International Online / E-Commerce**: Up to Rs. 250,000 equivalent in USD per day (subject to temporary 24-hour security activation toggle in Mobile App).
- **Monthly Account Limit**: Uncapped for verified tax-filers with active biometric records.

## 3. Bill Payment & Mobile Top-ups
- **Utility Bill Payments (1BILL)**: Up to Rs. 200,000 per transaction, Rs. 500,000 daily.
- **Mobile Balance Top-Up (EasyPaisa/JazzCash/Telco)**: Up to Rs. 20,000 per transaction, Rs. 50,000 daily.
