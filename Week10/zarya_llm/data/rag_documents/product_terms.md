# Zarya Digital Bank — Deposit Products & Terms of Service
Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Document Ref: PRD-2026-T1

## 1. Zarya Asaan Digital Current Account
- **Eligibility**: Pakistani resident citizens holding a valid computerized CNIC.
- **Minimum Initial Deposit**: Rs. 100 (No minimum monthly balance maintenance requirement).
- **Service Charges**: Zero monthly account maintenance charges.
- **Cheque Book Facility**: Optional 25-leaf cheque book available on request for Rs. 500.

## 2. Zarya Mahana Munafa Savings Account (Shariah-Compliant Mudarabah)
- **Profit Calculation**: Expected annual gross profit rate is **19.50% per annum**, calculated on daily average balance.
- **Profit Payout Frequency**: Credited directly to customer account on the 1st of each calendar month.
- **Withholding Tax & Zakat**: WHT deducted at 15% for active FBR tax-filers and 30% for non-filers. Statutory Zakat deducted on 1st Ramadan unless valid CZ-50 exemption declaration is submitted before Sha'ban 15.

## 3. Account Dormancy & Reactivation Rules
- **Dormancy Trigger**: Accounts with no customer-initiated financial debit or credit transactions for **12 consecutive months** automatically transition into Dormant status.
- **Reactivation Procedure**: The customer must perform biometric reverification through the Zarya Mobile App (via NADRA e-Sahulat API) or visit an authorized branch/service kiosk with original CNIC.
