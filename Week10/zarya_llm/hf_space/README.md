# Zarya Digital Bank: AI Production & Operations Platform

Interactive bilingual (UrduLish & English) customer banking assistant powered by **Qwen2.5-3B-DPO**, enterprise guardrails, RAG, and core banking ticketing.

## 🔗 Project Ecosystem Links
- **Production DPO Model**: [sairafatima193/zarya-qwen2.5-3b-dpo-v1](https://huggingface.co/sairafatima193/zarya-qwen2.5-3b-dpo-v1)
- **SFT Foundation Model**: [sairafatima193/zarya-qwen2.5-3b-sft-v1](https://huggingface.co/sairafatima193/zarya-qwen2.5-3b-sft-v1)
- **Banking Corpus & Test Set**: [sairafatima193/zarya-banking-corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/sairafatima193/zarya-banking-corpus)
- **Full Source Code**: [GitHub - SairaF-Dev/Netixsol (Week 10)](https://github.com/SairaF-Dev/Netixsol/tree/main/Week10)

## 🌟 Interactive Platform Modules
1. **Customer Banking Chatbot**: Natural UrduLish dialogue for card blocking, IBFT disputes, limits, savings rate, and SBP scam interception.
2. **Support-Agent Dashboard**: Real-time complaint queue with priority escalation, human handoff, and PII-masked audit logging.
3. **Model Evaluator**: Live side-by-side comparison between Base Qwen2.5-3B and Zarya DPO fine-tuned weights.
4. **Telemetry & Cost ROI**: Financial breakdown proving **91.4% cost savings** ($1,440/month saved) over US cloud APIs.
