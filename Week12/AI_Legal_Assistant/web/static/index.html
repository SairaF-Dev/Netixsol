03 · Contract review

Review a contract against the firm's playbook.

Each of the 14 playbook positions is rated acceptable, negotiate, unacceptable or missing, with the clause it relies on, a quote checked word for word, suggested wording and the statutory source.

The review appears here, with a Word file that carries each finding as a margin comment on the clause.

04 · Compare versions

See what the other side changed, and what it means.

Clauses are matched by content, not number, so a renumbered draft still lines up. Code finds every changed word, and the model only explains the changes the code found.

Deleted words appear struck through, inserted words underlined, each with its legal effect for your client.

05 · Drafting

First drafts, with every source checked.

Facts, issues, holding, reasoning and outcome of one judgment, each point tied to a paragraph and quote. Authorities come from the knowledge graph.

The legal basis is researched in the corpus and every citation passes the same checker as Research.

Starts from the firm's standard wording and adapts it to the deal, without weakening the firm's position.

Clauses

Every draft is headed “DRAFT — for lawyer review”. A citation that fails its check is removed and the text says so.
