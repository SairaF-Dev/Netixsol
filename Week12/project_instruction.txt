:loudspeaker: Week 12 Capstone: AI Legal Assistant
Day 1: Legal Document Ingestion & Understanding

Scenario: Our client Khan & Associates (law firm) has thousands of statutes, judgments and contracts, many of them scanned or in Urdu. Today you build the pipeline that turns these messy documents into clean, structured data.

Tasks
:one: Corpus Collection: collect at least 10 Pakistani laws, 200 judgments, 50 contracts (CUAD + 20 synthetic) and 30 scanned/Urdu documents. Create an inventory listing type, language, pages and source.
:two: Parsing & OCR: detect digital vs scanned PDFs, extract text and tables (Docling/PyMuPDF), and OCR scanned pages. Compare 2 OCR tools on 10 Urdu pages using CER.
:three: Structure Extraction: convert statutes (Act → Section), judgments (court, parties, citations, holding) and contracts (clauses) into JSON.
:four: Legal-Aware Chunking: compare fixed-size, section-level and parent-child chunking.
:five: Indexing: store chunks with metadata (Act, section, court, year, page, matter ID) in a vector DB and a BM25 index.
     
:package: Deliverable: ingestion pipeline code, OCR comparison and chunking comparison pushed to GitHub, with a short README.

:loudspeaker: *Week 12: Day 2: Advanced RAG & Citation-Grounded Legal Research*

Scenario: A lawyer asks "Agar consideration unlawful ho to kya contract void hai?" The answer must cite the exact section, and fake citations are not acceptable.

*Tasks*
:one: *Evaluation Set:* 100 questions (50 statute, 30 case-law, 20 with no answer in the corpus), each with its correct source.
:two: *Naive RAG Baseline:* measure Recall@5, MRR, faithfulness and citation accuracy.
:three: *Advanced Retrieval:* add the following one by one and record the improvement from each: hybrid search (BM25 + vector), reranking, query rewriting, HyDE, metadata filters and parent-child retrieval.
:four: *Cited Answers:* each answer must have a short answer, an explanation, exact citations (Act + section, or case + paragraph) and quoted source text. If no source is found, the answer must say "No sufficient source found".
:five: *Citation Verification:* automatically check that every cited section or case exists and every quote appears in its source. Target: *0 fabricated citations*.

:package: *Deliverable:* naive vs advanced RAG comparison table, plus a demo of 5 verified answers.


:loudspeaker: *Week 12: Day 3: Legal Knowledge Graph & GraphRAG*

Scenario: "Which Supreme Court judgments interpreted Section 23 of the Contract Act, and were any of them later overruled?" Questions like this cannot be answered from one paragraph, so today you build a knowledge graph.

*Tasks*
:one: *Graph Schema:* nodes (Act, Section, Case, Court, Judge, Party, Clause) and relationships (CITES, INTERPRETS, OVERRULES, FOLLOWS…). Draw the diagram.
:two: *Extraction:* extract entities and relationships with regex + LLM, and handle entity resolution. Measure precision and recall on 20 judgments.
:three: *Neo4j:* load the graph, write Cypher queries, run PageRank on citations, detect overruled cases and add a graph visualisation.
:four: *GraphRAG:* text-to-Cypher, plus local and global search (Microsoft GraphRAG, LightRAG or Neo4j GraphRAG).
:five: *Comparison:* on 30 multi-hop questions, compare Vector RAG vs GraphRAG vs Hybrid, then build a router that picks the engine automatically.

:package: *Deliverable:* a working Neo4j graph, GraphRAG demo and comparison report.


:loudspeaker: *Week 12: Day 4: Contract Review, Comparison & Drafting Agents*

Scenario: A client sends a 60-page agreement at 6 pm and needs comments by the morning. Today you build agents that review, compare and draft, so the lawyer only has to approve.

*Tasks*
:one: *Clause Classification:* extract and classify clauses using CUAD. Compare an LLM with a small fine-tuned model (Legal-BERT/DeBERTa) using F1 per clause type.
:two: *Playbook Risk Review:* mark each clause :white_check_mark: / :warning: / :x: against the firm playbook, list missing clauses, give a risk score and suggest wording. Export as a Word file with comments.
:three: *Contract Comparison:* compare 2 versions of a contract: show added, removed and changed text, and explain each legal impact in plain language.
:four: *Drafting Agent:* generate a case brief, a legal notice and contract clauses with sources, each marked "DRAFT — for lawyer review".
:five: *Long-Context vs RAG:* on 10 contracts, compare accuracy, missed clauses, cost and latency.

:package: *Deliverable:* a sample review report (.docx), comparison output and drafted documents.
